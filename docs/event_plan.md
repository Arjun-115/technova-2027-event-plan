### Lessons from Previous Editions (TechNova 2026)
1. **Registration bottleneck** — 200 people arrived in first 30 min. Fix: Pre-assign time slots.
2. **Wi-Fi overload** — Internet failed during coding contest. Fix: Dedicated LAN for competition labs.
3. **Food delay** — Lunch served 45 min late. Fix: Pre-order with caterer 3 days ahead; stagger lunch by event.
4. **Certificate errors** — 12 names misspelled. Fix: Double-check list 1 week before; participant self-verify via portal.
5. **Low social media coverage** — only 3,000 reach. Fix: Assign dedicated reels volunteer; post every 2 hours.

## 12. STUDENT REFLECTION

Having been involved in organizing the TechNova 2026 edition as a department
coordinator, the biggest learning was that **communication gaps cause 80% of
event-day problems**. We had all the plans on paper but the volunteer briefing
happened only 30 minutes before the event — nobody knew their exact station.

For TechNova 2027, I would enforce:
1. A **mandatory 3-hour volunteer orientation** one week before
2. A **WhatsApp broadcast group** specifically for day-of updates (no chit-chat)
3. A **15-minute morning muster** on event day before anything else opens

The best part of organizing an event is watching 800 strangers walk in, compete,
make friends, and leave with something they couldn't find in a classroom.
That's worth every late night spent designing the registration form.
