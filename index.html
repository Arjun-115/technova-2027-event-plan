Lessons from TechNova 2026

  1. Registration bottleneck — 200 people in first 30 min → Fix: Pre-assigned time slots
  2. Wi-Fi overload — Internet failed during coding contest → Fix: Dedicated LAN for labs
  3. Food delay — Lunch 45 min late → Fix: Pre-order 3 days ahead; stagger lunch
  4. Certificate errors — 12 names misspelled → Fix: Participant self-verify portal 1 week before
  5. Low social reach — Only 3,000 impressions → Fix: Dedicated reels volunteer + post every 2 hrs

💭 Student Reflection

"Having organized TechNova 2026 as a department coordinator, the biggest learning was that communication gaps cause 80% of event-day problems. All the plans were on paper but the volunteer briefing happened just 30 minutes before the event opened — nobody knew their exact station. For TechNova 2027, I would enforce a mandatory 3-hour volunteer orientation one week before, a dedicated broadcast-only WhatsApp group for day-of updates, and a 15-minute morning muster before anything opens. The best part of organizing an event is watching 800 strangers walk in, compete, make friends, and leave with something they couldn't find in a classroom."

— Arjun V B, 711523BCB009
